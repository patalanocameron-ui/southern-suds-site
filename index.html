FAQ

Pressure washing questions, answered.

What's the difference between pressure washing and soft washing?

Pressure washing uses high-pressure water to blast dirt off hard surfaces like concrete, driveways and pavers. Soft washing uses low pressure with a cleaning solution that kills mold, mildew and algae, which is the safe way to clean roofs, siding, stucco, screens and painted surfaces. Southern Suds uses whichever method is right for each surface.

How much does pressure washing cost in Palm Coast and St. Augustine?

Southern Suds gives every customer a free, competitive estimate based on the size of the property, the surfaces being cleaned and their condition. Customers often mention the price came in lower than other quotes they received. No job is too small. Call or text (386) 276-1451.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Southern Suds Powerwashing LLC is fully licensed and insured.

Do you need to use my water?

Usually not. Southern Suds brings its own water to the job, so you don't need to worry about your water bill or an outdoor spigot.

Will washing hurt my plants, paint or roof?

No. Delicate surfaces like roofs, siding, older aluminum siding and screens get a gentle soft wash, and plants are protected and rinsed so they aren't harmed. Customers have noted that nothing in their landscaping was damaged, even on heavily molded homes.

How often should I wash my house in Florida?

Most Palm Coast and St. Augustine homes need a house wash about once a year because heat, humidity and shade grow mold, mildew and algae quickly. Driveways, pool decks and lanais may need it more often, and roofs usually every few years.

Do you clean commercial properties?

Yes. Southern Suds pressure washes storefronts, gas stations, parking areas, sidewalks and other commercial concrete, and can schedule around business hours.

What areas do you serve?

Southern Suds Powerwashing serves Palm Coast, St. Augustine, Flagler Beach, Bunnell and the surrounding Flagler and St. Johns County area.
